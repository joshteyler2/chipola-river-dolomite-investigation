## Source types and caution

- Agency orders, permits, and inspections are primary regulatory records, but an inspector’s observation is not necessarily a final adjudication unless incorporated into an order.
- DMR values are self-reported unless identified as agency samples.
- Water Quality Portal and USGS files are machine-readable exports whose query scope/date matters.
- Sources 40–41 and 43–45 are researcher-created reproducibility/search notes linking back to original publishers or official systems; they are not agency publications.
- The advocacy reports documented by 40–41 support statewide context, not site-specific causation or motive.
