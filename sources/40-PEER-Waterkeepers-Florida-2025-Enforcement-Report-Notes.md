# PEER / Waterkeepers Florida 2025 enforcement-report notes

**Checked:** September 2, 2026

## Official source

- Report title: *Majority of Florida Waterways Fail Water Quality Standards as State Enforcement Remains Near Historic Lows: Florida Enforcement 2025 Report*
- Publishers: Public Employees for Environmental Responsibility and Waterkeepers Florida
- Publication date: July 2026
- [Official report page and download](https://peer.org/report-2025-florida-enforcement-report/)

The report is linked at its official host instead of being mirrored here. These notes provide stable page locators and the figures used in this repository.

## Figures used

On PDF pages 4–5, the report's statewide FDEP enforcement-action series shows:

| Measure | Reported value |
|---|---:|
| 2024 enforcement actions | 699 |
| 2025 enforcement actions | 755 |
| 1996–2010 annual average | 1,303 |
| 2011–2018 annual average | 431 |
| 2019–2025 annual average | 654 |

The report describes 2025 as an increase over 2024 while still below one-half of the 2010 level. The period averages are presented here as the publishers' calculations, not as independently reconstructed raw FDEP data.

## Use and limitations

- PEER and Waterkeepers Florida are advocacy organizations; the report is contextual analysis, not an agency adjudication.
- Statewide case counts do not establish staffing, inspection frequency, motive, or compliance at either Chipola facility.
- The report's “enforcement action” definition and source-data notes should be reviewed before comparing it with a differently defined agency metric.
- A separate [PEER page about the 2017 enforcement report](https://peer.org/florida-racks-up-second-worst-eco-enforcement-in-30-years/) supports the historical statement that FDEP initiated no mining enforcement cases in 2017. That is not a claim that current mining enforcement is zero.
