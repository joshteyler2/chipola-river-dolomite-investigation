## Limitations

- The database covers candidates for multicounty office and state political committees since 1996; county, municipal, and federal campaign records require separate searches.
- Contributor spelling, punctuation, DBA names, officer names, addresses, and data-entry errors can defeat a name search.
- An entity-name match should be checked against address/occupation fields before being treated as definitively the same business.
- A lawful disclosed contribution is not evidence of influence, favoritism, bribery, or a link to a permit decision.
- No search can prove the absence of contributions under untested names or in unsearched filing systems.

Recommended follow-up: request/search Jackson County Supervisor of Elections filings, City of Marianna filings, federal FEC records, relevant political-committee reports, and contributor variants tied to known officers and addresses.
