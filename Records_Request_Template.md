## 4. Information to attach to incident requests

- a marked map or GPS coordinates;
- first and last observation date/time;
- original photographs/video, not screenshots or social-media recompressions;
- species/count/size estimates and decomposition stage;
- whether live animals showed gasping, spinning, loss of equilibrium, seizures, unusual aggression, or lethargy;
- water color, odor, foam, sheen, temperature impression, weather, recent rain, and current direction;
- upstream and downstream limits searched; and
- every case number already issued by FWC, FDEP, or State Watch Office.
